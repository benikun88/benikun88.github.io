Narration

  1. I am Beni Kun. Here is the work.
  2. The focus is end-to-end automation, reporting, and CI for a multi-region SaaS platform. I keep the checks, the reports, and the pipeline in one system. That is the current role.
  3. The core is Python 3.13, pytest, and Playwright. I also use xdist, JUnit, GitLab CI, Kubernetes, S3, CloudFront, and Slack.

  6. A headed run on the public practice shop. Search, then filter, add to cart. The public sample passed.
  7. With Chrome DevTools MCP I inspect, choose locators, scaffold the test, run a check, and self-review. Sanity jobs keep artifacts.
  8. Appeals, driver client, flakes, this pipeline.
